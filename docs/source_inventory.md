## Interpretation limits

The source documents contain draft claims and naming inconsistencies. Read the [clarifications](study_progress.md#clarifications-before-implementation) and [evaluation protocol](data_and_evaluation_protocol.md) before implementing them. Reference photographs and diagrams do not establish that the team constructed a rig. Links to primary dataset and transform documentation are retained in the study summary and literature survey.

The reports are group work. Do not infer individual ownership of every result or claim experimental validation from the presence of a guide or a proposed workflow.
