## We built the paired dataset and increased feature detail

We used all 40 uploaded drive-end recordings across Healthy, Ball, Inner Race, and Outer Race. Each recording produced paired clean and noisy windows. The noisy variant used seeded white Gaussian noise with a target of 10 dB SNR per recording. We kept both versions and all overlapping windows from each original recording in the same split. Loads of 0–1 HP supplied 2,291 training pairs, 2 HP supplied 1,174 validation pairs, and 3 HP supplied 1,178 test pairs from 10 recordings. [Dataset layout](../../Datasets/Features/README.md) · [PR #7](https://github.com/M4Rk9/bearing-fault-diagnosis/pull/7)

After clarifying the request for more detail, we increased the Hann STFT frame from 256 to 512 samples and shortened the hop from 128 to 64 samples. At 12 kHz, this reduced frequency-bin spacing from 46.875 Hz to 23.4375 Hz and frame spacing from 10.67 ms to 5.33 ms. The longer frame spans 42.67 ms. We extended the db4 DWT from four to five levels. Each 2,048-sample CNN window now produces a 25 × 263 array, with 257 STFT bins and six DWT bands per frame. We regenerated the numeric dataset and 240 review PNGs showing the full retained recording alongside a one-second zoom. [PR #8](https://github.com/M4Rk9/bearing-fault-diagnosis/pull/8)
